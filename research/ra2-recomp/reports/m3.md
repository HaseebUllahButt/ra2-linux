# M3: one full skirmish exact (lifted against original)

Date: 2026-11-12. Run by the RECORDER on the test-host with the J8 replay tool (reports/j8, rec.dll) and rc.dll
(build/winv2: 19,273 lifted functions, RC_ON=all), loaded together by rclaunch.exe ('rec.dll;rc.dll').

## Result

- The lifted game replays the 5-minute recording rec-g (Isle of War, Russia vs America) to the end: 6,256 of 6,256 frames, no crash.
- Time calls match the original on every frame. Replay speed: about 400 frames/s lifted (19 times real speed).
- Lifted (RC_ON=all) against the control (rc.dll loaded, RC_ON=none): **0 differing value cells** in .data at frames 100, 3,000 and 6,256.
- Pointer cells (about 480 heap pointers) differ between any two runs; they are masked as noise.
- Lobby noise: the player-colour name table at 0xac1xxx and 5 cells (a72c80, a73438, a763d8, a763f4, ad045c) differ between two control runs too (none vs none).
- Four cells (8663fc, a324c4 from frame 1100; 8665b8, a32680 from frame 3000) are 7 in the plain original and 0xd in BOTH control and lifted runs.
  The cause is rc.dll being loaded, not lifted code. To check: rc.dll's stats thread (RC_NOSTATS=1).
